# 🩺 Core Medical Study Guide: Patient Assessment Basics

> **Stream:** Medical & Life Sciences | **Topic:** Clinical Vignettes & Examination

---

## 🎯 High-Level Overview
A structured clinical assessment ensures no critical symptoms are missed. It bridges basic medical science (anatomy and physiology) with real-world diagnosis and patient management.

* **Key Takeaway 1:** Always prioritize ABCDE (Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure) in acute settings.
* **Key Takeaway 2:** A thorough history often gives you 80% of the diagnosis before any lab tests or scans are ordered.

---

## 📖 Essential Terminology

| Term | Definition | Clinical Context / Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acute** | Sudden onset, short duration | Severe chest pain starting 10 minutes ago |
| **Chronic** | Long-term, slow progression | Mild joint stiffness over the past 3 years |
| **Differential Diagnosis** | A list of potential conditions that could explain symptoms | Considering pneumonia vs. bronchitis for a cough |

---

## 🔍 Step-by-Step Clinical Approach

### 1. History Taking
* **Chief Complaint:** In the patient's own words, what brought them in today?
* **History of Present Illness (HPI):** Onset, location, duration, characteristics, alleviating/aggravating factors, and severity (OPQRST).

### 2. Physical Examination
* **Inspection:** Look for visible signs (color changes, swelling, distress).
* **Palpation:** Feel for tenderness, masses, or abnormal temperature.
* **Auscultation:** Listen using a stethoscope (heart, lungs, bowel sounds).

## 🧠 Quick Review Questions

> **Q: What does the 'A' stand for in the ABCDE primary survey?**  
> **Answer:** Airway. Ensuring the patient's airway is patent and clear is always the absolute first step in emergency care.

> **Q: Why is taking a detailed history important before running lab tests?**  
> **Answer:** It helps narrow down differential diagnoses and guides you to order only the relevant tests, saving time and resources.
