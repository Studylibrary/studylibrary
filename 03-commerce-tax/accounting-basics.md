# 📊 Financial Accounting Fundamentals

> **Stream:** Commerce, Finance & Law | **Core Concepts**

---

## ⚖️ 1. The Accounting Equation
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owner's Equity}$$

* **Assets:** Resources owned by the company (Cash, Inventory, Equipment).
* **Liabilities:** Obligations or debts owed to outsiders (Loans, Accounts Payable).
* **Equity:** Residual interest in the assets after deducting liabilities (Capital, Retained Earnings).

---

## 🔄 2. Golden Rules of Accounting

| Account Type | Debit Rule | Credit Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Account** | Receiver | Giver |
| **Real Account** | What comes in | What goes out |
| **Nominal Account** | Expenses & Losses | Incomes & Gains |
